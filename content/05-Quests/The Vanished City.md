# The Vanished City

**Status: open.** The oldest thread, and the one nobody asked for.

## The question

[[Elturel]] is gone. Not sacked, not burned, not besieged. Gone, along with everyone in it, in a single night, leaving a shape in the landscape and a river of refugees heading downstream.

Nobody has explained it. Nobody official has seriously tried.

> [!broadsheet] THE BALDUR'S MOUTH
> ELTURAN QUESTION UNANSWERED AS COUNCIL MEETS THIRD TIME. The Council of Four concluded a third closed session this tenday without issuing a statement on the fate of Elturel or of Grand Duke Ravengard. Sources within the Citadel Streets describe the mood as "orderly." Refugee numbers at Rivington and Wyrm's Crossing are now past the capacity of the temples to feed, and the Guild of Watermen has declined further passage without payment in advance.

## What is established

- It happened a few weeks ago, at night, and there was a light
- There is no rubble and no crater
- Survivors are almost entirely people who were outside the walls that night
- **[[Ulder Ravengard]]**, Grand Duke of Baldur's Gate, was inside it
- Baldur's Gate has taken in a fraction of the refugees and left the rest outside the walls

## What is not established

Everything else, including whether it is over.

## Why it is your problem

Officially, it is not. Officially this is Elturel's tragedy, it happened somewhere else, and Baldur's Gate has enough to deal with.

Unofficially: the refugees are here, the Fist is stretched thin covering the gates, there are bodies turning up in the [[Lower City]] that nobody can account for, and the city is one bad week away from deciding the two facts are connected and doing something ugly about it.
