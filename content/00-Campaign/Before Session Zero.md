# Before Session Zero

Five questions. Answer them however you like: a sentence each is fine, a paragraph each is
welcome, and you can send them to me privately or drop them in the group chat.

None of this is a test and there are no wrong answers. It is the difference between me writing a
city you visit and me writing a city you already live in.

> [!tip] Answer these before you finish your character sheet, not after
> Three of the five will change what you build. It is much easier to pick a background that fits
> your answer than to reverse engineer an answer out of a background you already chose.

## 1. Where in the city are you?

A street, a neighborhood, a building, a stretch of dock. Anywhere on the
[[City Map|map of Baldur's Gate]] you like the sound of.

You do not have to be from here originally, but you do have to be **here now**, and you have to
have been here long enough that people know your face. See [[Campaign Primer]].

*What this gets you:* your character starts the campaign somewhere specific instead of nowhere.

## 2. Who here knows you?

**One person. Name them.**

Not a friend necessarily. A landlord, a rival, a former employer, someone you owe money to,
someone who owes you. Tell me who they are and one line about what they would say about you if I
asked them.

They do not have to be important. They will become important, because I will use them.

*What this gets you:* a face in the crowd who reacts to you by name.

## 3. What do you do for money?

The job, the hustle, the arrangement, the thing you are technically not supposed to be doing.
Whatever pays for the room.

If your [[Backgrounds|background]] already answers this, just say so and add one detail the
background sheet does not cover.

*What this gets you:* a reason to be somewhere at a specific hour, which is most of what an
adventure needs.

## 4. One bond with another player character

Required, and you sort it out **with them**, not with me. See [[Character Creation]].

You do not have to be friends. You have to be **familiar**. You have worked the same job, drunk
in the same room, been on opposite sides of the same argument, or one of you has been quietly
avoiding the other for a year. Any of that works.

*What this gets you:* a party that already has a reason to be in the room together.

## 5. Something from the past year you explained away

This is the one I actually care about most.

Think of one odd thing your character noticed in Baldur's Gate over the last year and decided was
nothing. A sound, a smell, a person, a street that felt wrong, a night you do not think about.
Something you had a reasonable explanation for at the time.

It should be small. It should be the kind of thing you would never bring up unprompted.

*What this gets you:* nothing yet.

## What happens to your answers

They become real. Everything you write down here is canon from session one, and I will put your
person, your street and your job in front of you at the table.

That last question especially. **Write it down honestly and then forget you told me.**
