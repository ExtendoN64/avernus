# Campaign Primer

## The Premise

Elturel is gone.

The city didn't fall, wasn't sacked, wasn't burned. It **vanished**: a light in the sky, and then no city, and now the refugees crowding the gates tell stories that agree on nothing except that light.

Everyone in **Baldur's Gate** is calling it a tragedy. A neighbor's horror, already over, nothing to do with them.

They couldn't be more wrong. It's **been** here.

A murder that's *wrong*, a cellar that's cold to the eye and scalding to the touch, a street the animals stopped walking down. It was already too late after everyone realized it wasn't stopping its momentum. Something has been bleeding up into the city from underneath, a little more every day, and Baldur's Gate has never handled a crisis gracefully, least of all one it doesn't know how to fight.

You unfortunate few are what the city has. You **are** the line. As long as it can be held, you'll have to go search after the thing doing this and put it out at the source.

You **need** to find out what happened to Elturel. Everything is making you feel like you're probably going to wish you hadn't.

## Tone & Expectations

This is a campaign about **infernal bargains and what people trade away when they're out of options**. Expect:

- **Moral compromise.** The devils here don't tempt you with simple evil. They tempt you with the *reasonable*. Contracts may or may not be the difference to your survival. _But at what cost?_
- **A grim setting with room to breathe.** It gets dark, but you're not powerless, and gallows humor at the table is welcome.
- **Consequences that stick.** Deals made will be honored: by the contractee, in the worst possible way, at the worst possible time.

## What This Campaign Is Not

- **Not a dungeon crawl.** Combat matters, but talking, scheming, and choosing badly matter more.
- **Not evil-party friendly.** As far as character goes, try to think of a reason to *not* take an easy deal. That's going to be the angle for _every_ PC here.
- **Not a solo story.** Everyone will start with a vague familiarity to each other, no complete strangers. _Unless okay 'ed by the DM, **BEFORE or DURING** Session 0._

## Characters

- **Starting level:** 1
- **Ancestry / class:** open, including allotted [[Homebrew|homebrew]]; other homebrew will be reviewed by the DM.
- **Stats:** see [[House Rules]]
- **Give me a hook into Baldur's Gate.** A debt, a family, a grudge, a job: something in this city that has an opinion about you. A drifter with no ties is a character I can't write toward.
- **Give me one bond with another PC.** Decide it together at _Session 0_.

## Session Zero Agenda

1. Pitch and tone check: is this the campaign you want to play?
2. [[Safety Tools]]: lines, veils, and how to pump the brakes
3. [[House Rules]]: mechanics, homebrew, expectations
4. Character concepts and party bonds
5. Scheduling and table logistics
6. Questions

## Table Logistics

- **Where:** This will be an online campaign. 
- **When:** At the mutually agreed schedule for everyone to agree on.
- **Session length:** 2 - 4 Hours per session.
- **Absences:** It would be preferable if no players are absent in sessions that will be important for the plot/development of the party. If consent is given to the party on the absentee, there will be a diligent effort to keep them as up to date as possible.
