# Starting Equipment

From **[ManBat's House-Rules](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PYAOb6NtrI53YKhW835HCzMt_QlHlwzRRsXxt--TvBk/edit?usp=sharing)**. More generous than the PHB, and you get it **in addition to** whatever your [[Backgrounds|background]] grants.

## By class

Pick one option from each line.

### Accursed
- Scale mail, **or** leather armour
- Hand crossbow and 10 bolts, **or** a martial weapon if proficient
- A hermit's, priest's, **or** scholar's pack
- Any spellcasting focus

### Alchemist
- Light crossbow and 10 bolts, **or** any simple weapon
- 40 gp of raw materials
- Leather armour **or** padded armour
- A dagger, 5 flasks, and alchemist's supplies
- A charlatan's, demolitionist's, **or** mediciner's pack

### Artificer
- Tinker's tools and a set of artisan's tools of your choice
- One simple weapon, one sidearm pistol and 10 rounds
- A hand cannon and 10 rounds, **or** a martial melee weapon
- Studded leather **or** scale mail
- A blacksmith's, burglar's, **or** demolitionist's pack

### Barbarian
- Any melee weapon with the **heavy** or **versatile** property
- Two handaxes **or** any simple weapon
- Hide armour and five javelins
- An explorer's, soldier's, **or** trapper's pack

### Bard

## New and changed gear

### Weapon properties you will not know

- **Heavy (X).** Disadvantage on attack rolls unless your Strength is at least X. This replaces the PHB version, and it applies to everyone rather than only Small creatures. A greataxe is **Heavy (15)**.
- **Kick (X).** Usable in one or two hands. Disadvantage on ranged attacks one handed unless your Strength is at least X.
- **Loud.** The shot is audible out to 300 feet.
- **Pocket.** Advantage on Sleight of Hand checks to conceal it.
- **Reload (X).** Fires X times before reloading. Reloading costs an action or a bonus action and needs a free hand.
- **Fist.** You may deal this weapon's damage instead of your normal unarmed strike damage.

> [!warning] DECIDE: are guns in Baldur's Gate?
> This is a setting call rather than a balance one. Firearms on the Sword Coast are usually rare, Gondian, and closely held, which is a very different thing from a gunslinger walking into the Elfsong with a rifle on their back.
>
> I am fine either way, but we should agree **before** anyone builds a Gunslinger or an Artificer. Options: fully in, in but rare and conspicuous, or out entirely. Session zero.

## Crafting

You can craft nonmagical items with tools you are proficient in, spending downtime and raw materials worth **half** the item's market value.

> [!warning] DECIDE: the source gives two different crafting rates
> ManBat's document contains both the 2014 PHB rule and the Xanathar's rule, and they disagree badly:
>
> | Rule | Rate | Plate armour (1,500 gp) |
> |---|---|---|
> | As written first | 5 gp per day | **300 days** |
> | As written second | 50 gp per day | **30 days** |
>
> A factor of ten apart. Until we settle it I will run **50 gp per day**, because 300 days of downtime for one suit of armour is not a real option in a campaign with a burning clock.
>
> Multiple characters can combine effort, each contributing their daily rate.

**Alchemy** has its own rules. See [[Homebrew]].
