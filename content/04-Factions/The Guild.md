# The Guild

Organized crime in Baldur's Gate, organized well enough that "crime" undersells it. The Guild runs smuggling, protection, gambling, fencing, and information, and it does so through a loose federation of crews rather than a single hierarchy.

Nobody outside it is certain who leads it. Plenty of people inside it are not certain either, and that is deliberate.

## How it actually works

The Lower City and the Outer City are carved into **crew territories**. A crew handles its own patch, pays upward, and settles disputes without involving the [[The Flaming Fist|Fist]]. Crews vary enormously: some are barely a dozen people running a few streets, some are effectively small businesses with an enforcement arm.

For most residents, the Guild is not an abstraction. It is the specific person you pay, and the specific person who deals with your problem when the Fist will not.

## The understanding

The Guild does not touch the patriars' persons or the Upper City's temples. In exchange, the Fist does not make a serious effort to dismantle it. Both sides break this quietly and neither side breaks it loudly.

The Guild also has a genuine rule against murder-for-spectacle. Bodies are bad for business. This is worth knowing right now, because the killings in the [[Lower City]] are exactly the kind of thing the Guild would normally stop, and it has not stopped them.

## What they want

Stability and volume. Refugees are, coldly, an opportunity: labor, desperation, and a great deal of movement through the gates. Some crews are exploiting that. A few are feeding people, because a fed neighborhood is a quiet neighborhood.
