# The Flaming Fist

The city's standing army, city watch, and mercenary company, all at once. Founded as a mercenary outfit and never quite stopped being one.

The Fist keeps the peace, garrisons the gates, patrols the roads, and hires itself out abroad when the city can spare the bodies. It is competent, expensive, and institutionally certain that it is underappreciated.

## Right now: it has no head

**Grand Duke [[Ulder Ravengard]]**, who commanded the Fist as its Marshal *and* sat as Grand Duke, went to [[Elturel]] and did not come back. Nobody has been formally raised to replace him, because doing so would concede that he is not coming back.

The practical result: every officer with ambition is auditioning, and every order comes with an unspoken question about who is actually giving it.

- **Commander Liara Portyr** has been recalled from Fort Beluarian in Chult by her uncle, Duke Dillard Portyr, to steady the Fist. Her arrival is a political act and everyone knows it.
- **Captain Zodge** holds the Basilisk Gate and is one of several officers keen to be noticed.

## What they are dealing with

- More people at the gates than the city has ever had to process
- Patrols pulled off the inner Lower City to cover the gates
- A string of killings they have not solved and have not fully disclosed

## How they treat you

Depends entirely on where you are standing and what you look like. In the Upper City they are polite. In the Lower City they are busy. In the Outer City they are barely present, and when they do appear it is usually in numbers and usually about something else.

They will conscript. The Fist has always had the right to press capable people into service during an emergency, and it is currently an emergency.

> [!notice] By Order of the Flaming Fist
> All able persons are subject to conscription for the duration of the present emergency. Refusal is a civil offense. Report to the Basilisk Gate.
