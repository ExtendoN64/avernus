# The Dead Three

**Bane**, **Bhaal**, and **Myrkul**. Tyranny, murder, and death. Three gods who died together long ago, came back, and have never once been out of fashion in this city.

Worshipping them is illegal in Baldur's Gate. It has been illegal for centuries, and there has never been a century without them.

## Why here

Baldur's Gate has a history with Bhaal specifically that it would prefer not to discuss at length, and a permanent supply of the two things the Dead Three feed on: people with grievances, and people with ambitions.

Their cults do not recruit through faith. They recruit through usefulness. Someone offers to solve your problem, the problem gets solved, and by the time you understand what you have joined you have already done something you cannot take back.

## The three, as the city understands them

| | Domain | Their followers, in practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Bane** | Tyranny, order through fear | Organized, disciplined, interested in control. The most dangerous long-term |
| **Bhaal** | Murder | Ritual killers. The ones that make the broadsheets |
| **Myrkul** | Death, the dead | Necromancy, corpses, and a patience the other two lack |

They are traditionally rivals. When they cooperate, historically, it has been bad.

## Right now

There have been killings in the [[Lower City]]. The [[The Flaming Fist|Flaming Fist]] has publicly confirmed one. The street has counted more.

What is circulating: the bodies are marked, the marks are ritual, and at least one temple has quietly told its people to stop travelling alone after dark.

> [!warning] What is not established
> Whether the marks are genuine, whether it is one group or several, and whether it has anything to do with [[Elturel]]. Everyone has a theory. Nobody has evidence.
