# The Patriars

The old noble families of Baldur's Gate. Not aristocracy in the sense of titles granted by a crown, but in the older and more durable sense: they have had the money for long enough that nobody remembers where it came from.

They own the Manorborn estates in the [[Upper City]], most of the shipping, and a great deal of the Lower City's ground rent. They are not universally hated, but they are universally resented, which is more stable.

## The Council of Four

The city's elected rulers, drawn in practice from patriar families. There are supposed to be four. There are currently three in the city.

| Duke | Status |
|---|---|
| **Grand Duke Ulder Ravengard** | Absent. Went to [[Elturel]]. No word |
| **Duke Belynne Stelmane** | In the city. Has been in poor health for years and rarely speaks at length |
| **Duke Dillard Portyr** | In the city. Has recalled his niece Liara from Chult to command the Fist |
| **Duke Thalamra Vanthampur** | In the city. Widely considered the most effective politician on the Council |

## Families

Thirty-odd families count as patriar. The names you hear most: Belt, Caldwell, Durinbold, Eltan, Eomane, Guthmere, Hhune, Jannath, Jhasso, Linnacker, Oberon, Provoss, Ravenshade, Sashenstar, Shattershield, Vanthampur, Whitburn.

Some are ancient and broke. Some are new and pretending not to be. The distinction matters enormously to them and to almost nobody else.

## What they want

Property values, continuity, and for this whole Elturel business to resolve itself without touching them.

To be fair to them, a few are genuinely trying to help with the refugees. To be fair to everyone else, they are a minority and they are losing the argument.
