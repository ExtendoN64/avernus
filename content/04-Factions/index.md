# Factions

Who holds what, and who is angry about it.

- **[[The Flaming Fist]]**, the city's army and police, currently leaderless
- **[[The Patriars]]**, old money and the Council of Four
- **[[The Guild]]**, organized crime, and the closest thing the Lower City has to a functioning government
- **[[The Dead Three]]**, murder cults, and the city's oldest recurring nightmare

## How power actually works here

Baldur's Gate has three power structures and none of them can remove the others.

The **patriars** own the property and hold the elected seats. The **Guild** controls the streets that the patriars' money has to travel through. The **Flaming Fist** answers to the Council on paper and to whoever is paying and present in practice.

Each of them can make life impossible for the other two, so mostly they leave each other alone. It is not a peace. It is a stalemate that everyone has agreed to call a peace, and it has held for a long time.

It is currently under more strain than it has been in living memory: the Grand Duke is missing, the Fist has no commander, and there are more people outside the walls than the city knows how to count.
