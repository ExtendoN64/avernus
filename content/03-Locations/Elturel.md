# Elturel

A city on the Chionthar, four days upriver. Devout, orderly, and famous for one thing.

**It is not there anymore.**

## What it was

Elturel was the seat of the **Hellriders**, an order of cavalry with a reputation for competence and a habit of riding out to help people who had not asked. It was governed by a **High Overseer**, currently **Thavius Kreeg**, and it was the most openly religious major city on the river.

Its defining feature was **the Companion**: a second sun, hanging over the city, that never set. It burned undead to ash in its light and made Elturel one of the safest places in the region to sleep outdoors. Elturans regarded it as proof of divine favor. Outsiders mostly regarded it as showing off.

## What happened

Nobody agrees.

What is not in dispute: on a night a few weeks ago, there was a light. Then there was no Elturel. No rubble, no crater, no bodies, no siege, no army. The city and, as far as anyone can tell, everyone in it, are simply gone, and there is a shape in the landscape where it used to be.

Survivors exist, mostly people who were outside the city that night, plus a number of refugees whose accounts of their own escape do not hold together under questioning. They have been arriving at Baldur's Gate ever since.

## What Baldur's Gate has done about it

Very little, loudly.

Grand Duke **[[Ulder Ravengard]]** was in Elturel when it happened, on a diplomatic visit at Thavius Kreeg's invitation. There has been no word from him.

## The rumors

Nobody knows which of these are true. Some are certainly not.

- The whole city was taken intact, buildings and people together, in a single night.
- The Companion went out, and everyone standing in its light went out with it.
- Baldur's Gate did it, to remove a rival on the river.
- Elturel is coming back, and it will not come back alone.
- The refugees brought something with them.

> [!warning] That last one
> The rumor that the refugees are carrying whatever happened is the one gaining ground fastest in the Outer City, and it is getting people hurt. Your characters will hear it. What they do about it is up to them.
