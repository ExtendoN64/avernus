# Upper City

Behind the **Old Wall**, on the high ground. Temples, the seat of government, and the families who have been rich long enough to consider it a personality.

The gates in the Old Wall close at dusk. Getting through them after dark requires a name, a writ, or a bribe, and the [[The Flaming Fist|Flaming Fist]] is noticeably better staffed up here than anywhere else in the city.

## Neighborhoods

| Neighborhood | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Wide** | The great market square and the city's public stage. Proclamations, festivals, executions. If Baldur's Gate wants to be seen doing something, it does it here |
| **Citadel Streets** | Government. The Flaming Fist's high command, the halls where the Council of Four meets, and a great deal of waiting in corridors |
| **Temples** | The temple district. Gond, Helm, Ilmater, Oghma, Siamorphe, Tymora, Umberlee, each with their own tone and their own opinion about the refugees |
| **Manorborn** | Patriar estates. Walled, staffed, and quietly furious about anything that lowers property values |

## What it feels like

Clean in a way that costs money. The streets are swept, the guards are polite to the right people, and there is a specific silence to the Manorborn streets at night that people from the Lower City find unnerving rather than peaceful.

## What people say about it right now

- The Council has met three times since Elturel and announced nothing.
- Someone up here decided the refugees are a Lower City problem.
- The temples are the only institution doing anything useful, and even they are running out of room.
