# Lower City

Between the Old Wall and the water. The harbor, the warehouses, the taverns, and most of the people. If Baldur's Gate has a heart, it is down here, and it works for a living.

Crowded, loud, and layered: the city has been built on top of itself so many times that half the cellars down here open into something older.

## Neighborhoods

| Neighborhood | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Bloomridge** | Respectable, striving, the closest the Lower City gets to comfortable |
| **Brampton** | Workshops and trades. Noisy at all hours |
| **Eastway** | The road out east. Traffic, inns, and everyone passing through |
| **Heapside** | Dense, poor, and stubborn |
| **Seatower** | The harbor's western arm, around the Seatower of Balduran |
| **The Steeps** | Steep. Stairs instead of streets in places |

## Gray Harbor

The reason the city exists. Ships from everywhere, cargo from everywhere, and a customs system that everyone involved understands to be theater.

## What it feels like

Smoke, fish, tar, and too many people. Sound carries strangely between the buildings. It is the friendliest part of the city and also the part where you keep a hand on your purse, and nobody down here finds those two facts contradictory.

## What people say about it right now

- The refugee camps are pushing into Heapside and nobody planned for it.
- The Flaming Fist has pulled patrols toward the gates, so the inner streets are thinner at night than they have been in years.
- There has been a murder. Or four, depending on who you ask and how drunk they are.

## Notable

- **The Elfsong Tavern**, a Lower City institution, named for the voice that sometimes sings in it. Nobody has ever satisfactorily explained the voice, and the regulars have stopped trying.
- **The Low Lantern**, a tavern on a permanently moored ship in the harbor. Gambling above, business below, and a policy of not asking.
