# Outer City

Everything outside the walls. It is not legally Baldur's Gate, it pays no city taxes, and it receives almost nothing in return. It is also where a great deal of the city's actual business happens, because the things Baldur's Gate needs and does not want to look at all end up out here.

Sprawling, unplanned, and largely unpoliced. The [[The Flaming Fist|Flaming Fist]] patrols the roads and the gates, not the neighborhoods.

## Neighborhoods

| Neighborhood | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Rivington** | The southern approach. Inns, markets, and the first thing travelers see |
| **Wyrm's Crossing** | The bridge settlement over the Chionthar. Built on and around the bridge itself |
| **Blackgate** | Hard, and named for the gate it huddles against |
| **Little Calimshan** | Calishite community, and the best food outside the walls |
| **Norchapel**, **Whitkeep**, **Twin Songs** | Working districts, each with their own temple loyalties |
| **Sow's Foot**, **Stonyeyes**, **Tumbledown** | Poor, crowded, and largely left alone |

## What it feels like

Improvised. Buildings lean on each other. Roads become alleys become somebody's roof. It is the most alive part of the city and the least protected, and the people here are extremely clear about which of those facts the Upper City cares about.

## What people say about it right now

- This is where the refugees are actually going, because the city will not let most of them in.
- The camps are past what the neighborhoods can feed.
- Somebody is going to blame the Outer City for the murders, and everyone out here knows it.
