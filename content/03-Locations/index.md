# Places

Everything your characters would know about the world by living in it. No secrets here, just geography, reputation, and the things people say.

> [!note] This page is common knowledge
> If your character grew up in Baldur's Gate, or has spent a season here, they know all of this. You never need to roll to remember something on this page.

## Baldur's Gate

A port city on the Chionthar, rich, crowded, and run by people who are very good at not being blamed for things. It is built in three parts, and which part you were born in tells everyone almost everything they think they need to know about you.

- **[[Upper City]]**, behind the Old Wall. Temples, government, and old money.
- **[[Lower City]]**, the harbor and everything that works. Where most people actually live.
- **[[Outer City]]**, outside the walls entirely. Where the city keeps what it will not admit it needs.

The city is governed by the **Council of Four** and policed by the **[[The Flaming Fist|Flaming Fist]]**, and the real power is split between the **[[The Patriars|patriars]]** and **[[The Guild]]**, who both pretend the other does not matter.

## Elsewhere

- **[[Elturel]]**, four days upriver. Or it was.

## The short version, if you are new here

Baldur's Gate does not have a ruler so much as a standoff. Money is old and hoarded in the Upper City, work happens in the Lower City, and everyone the city finds inconvenient lives outside the wall in the Outer City and is blamed for whatever goes wrong.

It has absorbed shocks before. It is absorbing one now, and doing it badly: the gates are choked with people who used to live in a city that no longer exists, and nobody in charge has said a useful word about it.
