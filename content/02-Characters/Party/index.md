# Party Roster

Who you are, and what the rest of the table should remember about you.

> [!note] Fill these in at session zero
> One page per character. Nothing mechanical is required here: no stat blocks, no build notes. This is the page another player checks when they forget your character's sister's name.

*Characters are created at session zero.*

## What goes on a character page

Keep it short. Six lines beats six paragraphs.

- **Name**, and what people actually call you
- **What you look like**, in one sentence somebody would use to describe you across a room
- **Your tie to Baldur's Gate.** Required. A debt, a family, a job, a grudge. Something in this city with an opinion about you
- **Your bond with another PC.** Required. How you know each other, and what you owe
- **What you want**
- **What you would not do for it**

## What does not go on a character page

Your secrets. If your character has something the rest of the table should not know yet, it does not go here. Send it to me and it lives in my notes until it comes out in play.
