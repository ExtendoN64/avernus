# Midas Tactus

**A cleric, by his own account.** Played by Khal.

## What people notice

He takes odd jobs around the city. Blessings, wards, sitting with the sick, the occasional
consecration nobody else wanted. Honest work, paid badly.

Ask him about himself and he will tell you he is **just a low level cleric**, in those words,
and change the subject. He says it often enough that people repeat it back to him.

## His brother

He had one. A **high paladin**, and by every account a considerable one.

He is dead. Midas does not volunteer how, and does not react well to being asked twice.

## Known to

- **The Muaser Brothers.** [[Tam]] and [[Seam]]. They have crossed paths on jobs more than
  once, being in the same trade. Not friends. Familiar.
- **[[Vedmaak Yaga|The Drifter]].** Midas heard wailing, followed it, and found Vedmaak at the
  bottom of a pit. He hauled him out, warned him about the area they were standing in, and went
  on his way. Vedmaak went further in.
