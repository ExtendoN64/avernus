# Private Handouts

Some pages on this wiki are **password protected**. They look like normal pages in the sidebar, but the content is encrypted until you type the password in.

If you have one, I gave you the password directly. It is yours. You do not have to share it, and nobody else at the table can read the page without it.

## What these are used for

- **Your character's private information.** Backstory you have not told the party, a secret you are keeping, a contact nobody else knows about.
- **Catching up.** If you miss a session, I can put a fuller recap behind a password so you get the detail without spoiling anyone who was there.
- **Split-party scenes.** What happened while the rest of the group was somewhere else.

## Ground rules

- Reading someone else's handout is not a fun table game. Do not go looking.
- If you lose your password, message me. There is no recovery on the page itself, I just resend it.
- You can always ask me to take a handout down.
