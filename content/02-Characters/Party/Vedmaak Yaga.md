# Vedmaak Yaga

**A wanderer.** Played by Juan.

## What people notice

He does not stay anywhere long, and he turns up in places nobody sensible goes.

The thing people remember about him is the warnings. They arrive unasked, they are strange,
and they are **specific in a way that is difficult to explain afterwards**. Not "the road is
dangerous." More like knowing which turning, and roughly when, and something about it that he
could not have been told.

He does not make much of this. Pressed on how he knows, he says something that is not really
an answer, and moves on.

## How he carries himself

Deeply superstitious, and rigorous about it. He reads meaning into things other people walk
past, keeps rules nobody else has heard of, and takes his own dreams seriously enough to
rearrange a day around one.

He also has an unhelpful habit: having been warned off somewhere, by himself or anyone else, he
tends to go there.

## Known to

- **Irish-Natural.** Fellow traveller. It began with an unsolicited warning from Vedmaak that
  turned out to be worth having. It became a habit: two people always on the move, crossing
  paths often enough to trade what they have each seen.
- **Fools-Gold (Midas).** Midas found him at the bottom of a pit, in a cave he had walked into
  on purpose. Midas got him out, warned him off the woods nearby, and left. Vedmaak went into
  the woods immediately.

> [!warning] The drifter problem
> [[Campaign Primer]] is blunt: *"A drifter with no ties is a character I can't write toward."*
> A wanderer who blew into town is the one shape this campaign struggles with, because Act II
> puts a district he cares about in danger.
>
> Arriving because of a hunch is a reason to arrive, not a reason to stay. He needs **a person**.
> Some options that keep the concept intact:
>
> - Someone he warned years ago who **listened**, lived, and settled here owing him everything.
> - Someone he warned who **did not listen**. Their family is in this city, and they know his name.
> - A name he was given long ago, with instructions, that he has been putting off.
