# Handout: Example

You unlocked it. That is all this page exists to demonstrate.

The password for this one is `avernus`, and it is written openly on [[Private Handouts]] because this is a demo. A real handout's password would only ever be sent to you directly.

## What a real one would hold

- Something your character knows that the party does not
- A letter, contract, or map only you were given
- A private recap of a scene the rest of the table was not in
- The part of your backstory you are not ready to say out loud

## How it works, briefly

The page is encrypted before it is ever uploaded. What sits on the web server is unreadable without the password, so this is not "hidden," it is genuinely locked.

Practical limits worth knowing:

- The strength of the lock is the strength of the password. Short or guessable means weak.
- Once a version is published, that encrypted copy exists. Changing the password later does not retract the old one.
- So: good for table privacy, not for anything that would genuinely harm you if it got out.
