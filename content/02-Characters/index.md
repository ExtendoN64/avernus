# Characters

- **[Party Roster](Party/)**, who you are
- **[People](NPCs/)**, who you have met and what you know about them

The People pages record **what your characters know**, not what is true. If an NPC has lied to you, the lie is what gets written down until you find out otherwise. That is on purpose.
