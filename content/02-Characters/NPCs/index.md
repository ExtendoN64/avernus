## Public figures

Anyone living in Baldur's Gate knows these names.

- **[[Ulder Ravengard]]**, Grand Duke and Marshal of the Flaming Fist. Missing in [[Elturel]]
- **Duke Belynne Stelmane**, of the Council of Four. In poor health, rarely seen
- **Duke Dillard Portyr**, of the Council of Four. Recalled his niece Liara from Chult
- **Duke Thalamra Vanthampur**, of the Council of Four. The Council's most capable operator
- **Thavius Kreeg**, High Overseer of Elturel. Invited Ravengard. Fate unknown
