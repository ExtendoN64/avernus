# Ulder Ravengard

**Grand Duke of Baldur's Gate. Marshal of the [[The Flaming Fist|Flaming Fist]]. Missing.**

## What everyone knows

Ravengard came up through the Fist rather than down from a patriar family, which is the single most important fact about him and the one he has never let anyone forget. He made Marshal on competence, then took a seat on the Council of Four, then Grand Duke.

He is not liked by the [[The Patriars|patriars]]. He is respected by the Fist. He is regarded by most of the [[Lower City]] as the only person in the Upper City who has ever been on their side, which is a reputation he has earned unevenly but earned.

Blunt to the point of rudeness. No patience for ceremony. Widely considered incorruptible, which in Baldur's Gate reads to some people as a character flaw.

## Why he was in Elturel

He was invited by **Thavius Kreeg**, Elturel's High Overseer, for talks between the two cities.

He did not want to go. It reportedly took months of persuasion from the other three dukes to get him on the road, and he left with the Fist's command structure only partly delegated, because he expected to be back inside a month.

He was in [[Elturel]] when it vanished.

## Now

No word. No body. No confirmation of anything.

The Council has not declared him dead, which means the Fist has no Marshal and no legal way to appoint one. Commander **Liara Portyr** has been recalled from Chult to hold it together, and the Fist's officers are variously loyal to Ravengard's memory, to whoever ends up in charge, and to themselves.
