Colour tokens

The complete palette. These nine names are Quartz's, set in quartz.config.yaml under configuration.theme.colors, and every rule in custom.scss reads them rather than hard-coding a value. Changing a token here changes the whole site, including the city map.

In dark mode the names invert semantically: light is the dark page ground and dark is the pale text. That is Quartz's convention, not a mistake. The trap it sets: writing a rule against dark expecting a dark colour gives you a pale one in dark mode.
