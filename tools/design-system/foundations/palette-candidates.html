The stock Claude themes shipped with Quartz were rejected on inspection rather than taste: the one called claude defines dark mode only, and the site has a light and dark toggle, so light mode would fall through unstyled. claude-warm has both modes but its accent is a gold, not Claude's clay. Both alternatives above are therefore hand-built into the config's own colour block, which also avoids the 740 KB stylesheet the plugin themes carry.
