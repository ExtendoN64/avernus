Callout: contract

An infernal bargain, reproduced as written. This is the one component with a job beyond legibility: it should make the reader uneasy. House rule is that any deal offered is real and will be honoured exactly as written, so the horror has to come from the terms, not from decoration.

light
Terms of Agreement
  1. The party of the first part shall provide safe passage, without delay or condition, to the bearer.
  2. The party of the second part shall provide, at a time of the first part's choosing, one service.
  3. The nature of the service is not specified herein and shall not be refused.
  4. This agreement is binding upon signature and is not dissolved by the death of either party.

Tokens: border and title secondary, wash highlight over light, body inherits darkgray.
Rules: monospaced body, because a contract is typed, not written. Numbered clauses always, even for a single term. 1.7 line height, which is looser than anything else on the site and is what makes it read as a document rather than a quote. The diagonal wash is the only gradient in the system.
Warning: this component is the first casualty of a calmer palette. It works because secondary is an alarming colour. Swap in a softer accent and the clauses stop landing.
