Markup: a <div class="hero"> or <div class="card-grid"> wrapped around ordinary Markdown. Blank lines are required around the div for the Markdown inside to parse, but never inside raw HTML blocks: a blank line there terminates the block and orphans everything after it into stray paragraphs.
Hero: Cinzel at 1.15rem, centred, text-wrap: balance. Rules top and bottom in secondary, with a rotated square straddling the top rule. The gradient fades out by 60% so the text never sits on a tint.
Cards: the first card above shows the hover state: left border moves from secondary to tertiary, a 2px lift, and a soft shadow. Minimum column 15rem, so the grid drops to one column on a phone rather than crushing.
